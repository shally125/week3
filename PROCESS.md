# Process

## Tools
I used the HKSAR Government's greenhouse gas JSON as the measurement source. Python's `json` module parses the cached reply, and Matplotlib transforms the records into a polar artwork. I used Codex to help interpret the assignment and inspect the source structure. I reviewed the output image and changed the composition so that all labels remained legible.

## Kept

I kept the template's separation between fetching and plotting. I kept all 35 years and all three measurements. Each year becomes a petal, with total emissions mapped to length, per-capita emissions mapped to width, and carbon intensity mapped to colour. This makes every visible petal a consequence of the published numbers. 

## Rejected

I rejected my earlier three-panel line chart. It was accurate, but it followed the designer's path and looked like a statistical report rather than treating the numbers as visual material. I also rejected random particles and decorative leaf textures because their shapes would not have been determined by the measurements.

The circular flower sacrifices exact lookup, but its length, width, colour, order, and outer contour all have defined relationships to the data.
